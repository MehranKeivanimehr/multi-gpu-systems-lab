# RTX A6000 study

Mehran Keivanimehr · qualified closeout, October 2026

This study combines historical training/serving records with later numerical controls at one and two GPUs. Its conclusions are claim-specific, not certification of every framework, model or historical run. [A6000 records (2026/10), [28-claim ledger](../results/a6000_validation/evidence/70_final_review/CLAIM_LEDGER_v2.csv)].

## Serving at an equal request budget

![Matched-cap serving](../assets/figures/a6000_serving.svg)

At aggregate concurrency 512, DP2 records 15,736.2 output tokens/s versus TP2's 11,810.9 for Qwen2.5-1.5B (+33.23%). The historical Llama8B comparison is 3,565.4 versus 3,100.7 (+14.99%). Each arm has two repeats. The means are descriptive; they are not confidence intervals or a universal deployment recommendation. Output/reference agreement was not tested on A6000. [Source records (2026/10), [cohorts](../studies/a6000/results/rerun/vllm_tp_concurrency_comparison.csv), [run IDs](../results/showcase.json)].

## Numerical calibration

![Calibration margins](../assets/figures/a6000_calibration.svg)

The FP32 DDP positive control compares five updates at world size one/accumulation four with world size two/accumulation two. All six fixed-slice endpoints pass: gradient relative L2 about 5.23e-8, maximum relative loss difference 1.95e-8, and five-update delta relative L2 1.01e-5. Four arrays totaling 12.6 MB make the original slice endpoint replayable on a CPU. [Control evidence (2026/10), [arrays and manifest](../results/a6000_validation/evidence/70_final_review/calibration_slices/), [original comparator result](../results/a6000_validation/evidence/44_c18_comparison/comparison_c18b.json)].

## Precision-policy investigation

![Precision contrast](../assets/figures/a6000_precision.svg)

The matched TF32-off/math-SDPA test extracts a first-boundary gradient without an optimizer update. Native BF16 and DeepSpeed BF16 have identical recorded hashes over 338 tensors and both differ from FP32 by 0.2175 relative L2. Autocast with FP32 parameters differs by 0.0362. The contrast reproduces the deviation without DeepSpeed under this policy; it does not isolate every precision component or establish later-update behavior. [Contrast evidence (2026/10), [per-tensor records](../results/a6000_validation/evidence/48_c20_fb_summary/out/fb_summary.json)].

Earlier S1a recipe comparisons use one GPU and three updates, not the original two-GPU/five-update design. They use full-vector rather than recoverable original-prefix grading, with prior pilot results visible. DDP/FSDP2 have recorded G1 relative errors 0.0399/0.0496; ZeRO-1/2 are about 0.218 and have cosine below the pass limit. These are informed follow-up results, not silent fulfillment of the original protocol. [Protocol disclosure (2026/10), [T13 and related claims](../results/a6000_validation/evidence/70_final_review/CLAIM_LEDGER_v2.csv)].

## Historical repeat analysis

Across 28 historical training groups with three records each, median sample CV is 0.15% and median range/median is 0.29%. Sixteen one/two-GPU non-8B groups are separated from twelve four-GPU/8B groups. Repeat dispersion describes these records; it does not retrospectively establish their gradient validity. [Historical data (2026/10), [per-group values and source paths](../results/corrections/a6000_repeats.csv)].

## Limitations

ResNet-50 agreement with FP32, A6000 inference output correctness and complete two-GPU validation across every framework remain unestablished. Four-GPU/8B training and ZeRO-3 validation are deferred. Sampled AdamW checks do not cover the whole model. Full-vector attribution payloads remain private/on-node; their index/hash/aggregate records are public. Energy, latency headlines and causal bottleneck claims are not promoted. See [the complete scope statement](LIMITATIONS.md).
